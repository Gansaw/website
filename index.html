About Me

Hello! I'm Hojin Choi, a flight data analyst and AI engineer in the aerospace industry. I am passionate about intelligent systems, machine learning, and solving real-world problems. I specialize in analyzing complex datasets, building predictive models, and designing intuitive solutions that connect data with innovation.

I believe that the approach of agents learning optimal strategies through interaction with their environment holds tremendous potential for solving complex challenges in the aviation domain.
